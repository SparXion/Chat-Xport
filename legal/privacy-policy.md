# Privacy Policy — SparXion Chat Xport

**Effective date:** June 25, 2026  
**Last updated:** June 25, 2026

SparXion (“we,” “us,” or “our”) publishes **SparXion Chat Xport**, a browser extension and companion app that helps you export AI chat conversations to Markdown files on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how the product handles information.

**Summary:** SparXion Chat Xport is designed to be **local-first and privacy-first**. We do **not** collect, store, sell, or transmit your chat content to SparXion or any other server. Export happens entirely on your device.

## 3. How the product works (local processing)

When you use SparXion Chat Xport on a supported AI chat website (such as Grok, Claude, Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini, Meta AI, or Mistral), the extension:

1. **Reads** the conversation content displayed in your browser tab (via the page’s DOM—the same text you see on screen)
2. **Formats** that content as a Markdown file on your device
3. **Saves** the file to your browser or system Downloads folder when you choose to export

All of this processing happens **locally on your computer or device**. Your conversation text is not sent to SparXion.

The extension only runs on AI chat sites you visit and only when you interact with it (for example, by clicking **Download Chat (markdown)**).

## 4. Permissions and why they are used

### Chrome / Chromium extension

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| **Host access** to supported AI chat domains | Inject the export UI and read conversation content on pages you open |
| **scripting** / **activeTab** | Run the export script in the active tab when you use the toolbar action (e.g. Perplexity) |
| **Extension resources** (e.g. Comet help page) | Show local help content bundled with the extension; no remote content loading |

### Safari extension (macOS / iOS)

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| **Access to supported websites** | Same as above: read visible chat content and offer export on sites you visit |
| **Downloads** (where applicable) | Save exported Markdown files to your Downloads folder |

We request the **minimum access needed** to perform export on supported sites. The extension does not run on unrelated websites.

## 5. Data stored on your device

SparXion Chat Xport does **not** maintain a cloud library of your chats.

- **Exported `.md` files** are saved where your browser or operating system stores downloads (typically your Downloads folder). You control those files.
- The extension does **not** persist your conversations in extension storage for later retrieval by SparXion.
- Browser and OS logs (outside our control) may record that a file was downloaded.

If you use the optional **Comet / Perplexity console workaround**, you paste a script into your browser’s developer console. That script runs as page JavaScript on your device and follows the same local-only export behavior.
