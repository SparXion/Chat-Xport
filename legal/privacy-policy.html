SparXion (“we,” “us,” or “our”) publishes SparXion Chat Xport, a browser extension and companion app that helps you export AI chat conversations to Markdown files on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how the product handles information.

Summary: SparXion Chat Xport is designed to be local-first and privacy-first. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit your chat content to SparXion or any other server. Export happens entirely on your device.

2. Information we do not collect

SparXion Chat Xport does not:

  • Require an account or login
  • Collect your name, email, or contact information through the extension or app
  • Upload your AI conversations to SparXion or third-party analytics services
  • Use advertising, tracking pixels, or marketing analytics
  • Sell, rent, or share your data for advertising or data-broker purposes
  • Use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending

We do not operate backend servers that receive your chat exports.

3. How the product works (local processing)

When you use SparXion Chat Xport on a supported AI chat website (such as Grok, Claude, Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini, Meta AI, or Mistral), the extension:

  1. Reads the conversation content displayed in your browser tab
  2. Formats that content as a Markdown file on your device
  3. Saves the file to your Downloads folder when you choose to export

All processing happens locally on your device. Your conversation text is not sent to SparXion. The extension only runs on AI chat sites you visit and when you interact with it.
