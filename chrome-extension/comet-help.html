Comet blocks extensions on Perplexity

Perplexity Comet adds perplexity.ai to an internal runtime_blocked_hosts list. Third-party extensions (including Adobe Acrobat, this extension, and others) cannot inject scripts on Perplexity pages inside Comet — even though they work in Chrome.

This is a Comet browser policy, not a bug in SparXion Chat Xport.

Workaround: run export from DevTools console

  1. In Comet, open your Perplexity thread (sidecar or full tab).
  2. Press F12 (or ⌥⌘I) → Console.
  3. Click Copy export script below, paste into the console, press Enter.
  4. A download button appears, or the export runs immediately.

Alternative

Use Google Chrome (not Comet) for Perplexity exports — the extension button works normally on perplexity.ai/search/....
